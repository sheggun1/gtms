About us

A school where every child is known by name

Greater Tomorrow Model Schools is a co-educational secondary school in Ado Ekiti, Ekiti State, where children are guided to grow in knowledge, in character and in confidence.

Our dedicated teachers and well-kept classrooms give every student the space to discover what they are good at.

Academic excellenceCareful teaching and steady preparation for WAEC and NECO.
Character formationDiscipline, integrity and responsibility in everything we do.
Leadership developmentStudent council, clubs and service programmes that build confidence.

For parents

What you can expect from us

We know you are trusting us with the most important person in your life. Here is how we try to earn that trust every day.

A safe, caring environment

Your child is known by name. Teachers and the school office look out for every pupil from morning to closing.

Teaching that works

Committed teachers, clear lesson plans and regular assessment, with extra help for those who need it.

Character first

Discipline, honesty and respect are taught daily, in the classroom, on the field and in how we speak to each other.

Doors always open

Results and school news are shared openly. Call, send a message or walk in. We are glad to hear from you.
